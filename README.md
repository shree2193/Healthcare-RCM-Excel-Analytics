# 🏥 Healthcare Revenue Cycle Analytics Dashboard (Excel Online)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project simulates a real-world Healthcare Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) system using Excel Online.  
It analyzes the full patient-to-payment workflow including encounters, claims, payments, and denial patterns to evaluate financial and operational performance.

The goal is to demonstrate healthcare analytics skills relevant to roles such as Healthcare Data Analyst, Revenue Cycle Analyst, and Medical Billing Analyst.

---

## 📊 Business Problem
Healthcare organizations face challenges in:
- Claim denials and revenue leakage
- Slow reimbursement cycles
- Lack of provider performance visibility
- Poor financial tracking across payers

This project addresses these issues using structured data analysis in Excel.

---

## 🧱 Dataset Structure

### 1. Patients
Demographic and insurance information

### 2. Encounters
Patient visits including OP/IP/ER types and visit costs

### 3. Claims
Insurance billing records with claim status (Paid / Denied)

### 4. Payments
Insurance reimbursement transactions and cash flow tracking

### 5. Master Data Tables
- ICD-10 diagnosis codes  
- CPT procedure codes  
- Provider details  
- Insurance payer information  
- Department mapping  

---

## 📈 Key KPIs Developed

- Total Claims Processed  
- Paid vs Denied Claims  
- Denial Rate (%)  
- Total Billed Amount  
- Total Paid Amount  
- Collection Rate (%)  
- Monthly Revenue Trend  
- Provider Performance (Revenue & Encounters)
